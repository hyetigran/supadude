Loop a FAST, energetic superhero sprint in place — a dash, not a jog. Torso leans forward about 15 degrees with the head leading. Quick cadence: about three full strides per second.

Legs: big stride with high knee drive. The front knee lifts to hip height, and the back leg kicks out straight behind with the foot well off the ground. Include a brief airborne moment where both feet are off the ground. The two legs must stay clearly separated in every frame. Never draw them crossing or merging into one line. At the passing pose, the lifted knee is clearly forward and bent.

Arms: both arms always visible and pumping hard, opposite to the legs. When the left leg is forward, the right arm is forward. Bent elbows, fists punching forward and swinging back past the hips. One arm in front of the torso, one behind it.

Cape: streams straight back horizontally from the neck with a quick flutter, never hanging down.

Small vertical bob: the body is lowest when a foot plants and highest during the airborne moment. No horizontal drift.

Keep the side camera locked, facing right, identical head size, thin stick limbs, facial features, outline weight and red cape attachment. Preserve the chroma background. No zoom, camera movement, scenery, extra characters, text, powers, speed lines, dust or particles. Keep the character inside the frame with padding. Movement through the level is controlled by the game: do not animate the character travelling across the screen.

Match the approved art style: clean bold rounded black outer outlines, sparse interior lines, rounded shapes, flat sunny colors and one broad cel-shadow patch. Keep the cleaner cartoon detail level. No textures, hatching, halftone, photorealism, 3D, lettering, logos or watermark.
